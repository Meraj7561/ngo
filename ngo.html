Why Aasha exists

Two generations. One promise.

On the same street, a nine-year-old girl is pulled out of school to work, while an eighty-year-old man eats his only meal of the day from a bin. Both have people who love them. Both have been forgotten by systems that should have held them.

Aasha exists to close that gap — with tuition, uniforms, notebooks and mid-day meals for children, and hot freshly cooked food, medicine and companionship for elders who have no one left. No middlemen, no politics — just neighbours helping neighbours.

Every rupee, dollar and euro you give becomes a plate of food or a seat in a classroom within seven days. We publish the receipts.
